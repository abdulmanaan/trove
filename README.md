# Trove

A home inventory app that helps homeowners record their belongings and check if their insurance actually covers them.

## The problem

When a fire, flood or theft happens, insurers ask what you owned, what it cost and where the proof is. Most people can't answer.

- Only 20% of insured homeowners created or updated a home inventory in the past year, and 25% have never made one (APCIA / Harris Poll survey of 1,204 US homeowners)
- APCIA estimates that 2 in 3 homes may be underinsured
- After Colorado's Marshall Fire, 74% of policyholders were found underinsured

## What Trove does

- Add homes, rooms and items with photo, price, purchase date and receipt
- See the total value of each room and the whole home
- Enter your insurance limit and see how much you're underinsured
- Coverage bar that shows what percentage is covered
- Alert when a new item pushes your total over the limit
- Yearly reminder to review your insurance limit
- One-click claim-ready report
- Sign up with email or Google

## Tech stack

- Frontend: React, Motion
- Backend: Node.js, Express
- Database: MongoDB
